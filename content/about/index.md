# About Me

I am Nami.

I was born in Japan and studied in the Philippines and the US. After 9 years living in the US, I moved to the Netherlands, where I feel a bit happier paying taxes.

My experience working with different people from different socioeconomic backgrounds taught me two things: the injustice and inequality in the world and the power of openness to make it better.

I'm interested in things related to openness, including open science, open data, open software, and open communication.
